Typography

  • Body: -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Helvetica, sans-serif. Size 15-16px, line-height 1.55.
  • Accent phrases: Georgia italic in orange #E97A2E inside serif headlines.
  • Kickers/labels: uppercase, letter-spacing 0.14em, size 11px, color #E97A2E.
  • Numbers (section 01, 02, 03): Georgia serif, size 32-40px, color #D9CFB8.

Buttons

  • Secondary: black #0F0D0A background, cream #FBF6EA text, pill radius.
  • Ghost: transparent, orange border 1.5px, orange text.

Global feel

Editorial magazine layout. Long-form serif. Cream background with orange accents. Numbered sections with kicker labels. Feels closer to a New Yorker article than a SaaS landing page. Reference: crackclay.co/campaigns.

10. Methodology (The CrackClay System)

METHODOLOGY

Section headline

How the 2-person team ships on your stack.

5-step numbered list

  1. Data. Google Maps extraction for owner-level inboxes that don't exist in Apollo, ZoomInfo, or Cognism. Delivered into your Clay.

12. FAQ

Kicker

FAQ

Section headline

Everything you'd normally ask on a call.

Accordion format. All questions and answers below.

How does the 3-day trial work?

Day 0 is a 45-minute kickoff where we scope one workflow in writing. Day 1 is build day (no meeting). Day 2 is a 60-minute working session where we demo the live workflow. Day 3 is a 45-minute handover where you receive the files, prompts, and a 90-day roadmap. Total meeting time: 2.5 hours across 3 days.

What tools do you integrate with?

Anything in your existing stack. Clay, Apollo, Instantly, Smartlead, PlusVibe, Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Google Workspace. We build on Claude Code + MCP so integration is prompt-driven, not code-heavy.

Do we own the workflows and files?

Yes. Fully. Every workflow file, prompt, and configuration lives in your environment. If we part ways, nothing walks out with us. You keep running everything we built.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. Tier 2 and Tier 3 are monthly, no long-term contract. Cancel at any point. All work already delivered stays with you.

What happens if we don't get results?

Tier 2: first workflow live inside 7 days from kickoff or month 1 is free. Tier 3: 3+ workflows per month verified through your project tracking or month 2 is free. The trial itself is free with no obligation.

Do you provide mailboxes or sending infrastructure?

No. This is intentional. We operate your existing stack. You already own the sending infrastructure (mailboxes, domains, sequencer). We bring the workflows, the methodology, the execution. Nothing walks in from our side.

How does your team access our stack?

Minimum required for the trial: Clay guest seat, your ICP definition (one paragraph), name of your sequencer, and a Claude Code collaborator seat. For Tier 2 and 3, we may request read access to your CRM and Slack channel access for async communication. No admin credentials ever needed.

Is this compliant with our SaaS security policies?

Access is scoped and revocable. Everything runs in your environment. We do not export data outside your systems. If you need us to sign an NDA or MSA before kickoff, we do. If you need us to work under your existing security review, we do.

Who is on my account?

Hassan (Founder) and one GTM engineer. Two people, always. Every call has both, every workflow build has both. If one is unavailable, the other covers. That's the redundancy the ICP loses when they hire a single GTM engineer.

How is this different from an AI SDR tool (11x, Artisan, Aurasell)?

AI SDR tools are products you rent to send emails. We are a team you rent to build the workflows behind whatever sending infrastructure you already run. You keep control of the copy, the sending cadence, the reply routing. We build the data + signal + personalization layer on your stack.
