  1. Data. Google Maps extraction for owner-level inboxes that do not sit in Apollo, ZoomInfo, or Cognism. Delivered into your Clay.

12. FAQ

Kicker

FAQ

Headline

Everything you would ask on a call.

Accordion format. 10 questions below.

How does the trial work?

Day 0 is a 45-min kickoff where we agree scope in writing. Day 1 is build day, no meeting. Day 2 is a 60-min working session with a live demo. Day 3 is a 45-min handover with files and a 90-day roadmap. Total meeting time across 3 days: 2.5 hours.

What tools do you work with?

Anything in your existing stack. Clay, Apollo, Instantly, Smartlead, PlusVibe, Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack. Everything is built on Claude Code + MCP so integration is prompt-driven, not code-heavy.

Do we own the workflows and files?

Yes. Every workflow file, every prompt, every configuration lives in your workspace. If we part ways nothing walks out with us. You keep running.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. Tier 2 and Tier 3 are monthly with no long-term contract. Cancel at any point. Everything already delivered stays with you.

What happens if we do not get results?

Tier 2: first workflow live in 7 days or month 1 refunded. Tier 3: 3+ workflows shipped per month verified through your project tracking or month 2 refunded. The trial is free with no obligation.

Do you provide mailboxes or sending infrastructure?

No. This is deliberate. You already own the sending stack. We operate it, we build the workflows behind it, we do not layer our infrastructure on top of yours.

How does your team access our stack?

For the trial: Clay guest seat, ICP paragraph, sequencer name, Claude Code collaborator seat. For Tier 2 and 3: read access to your CRM and a Slack channel for async. No admin credentials ever needed.

Is this compliant with our security review?

Access is scoped and revocable. We do not export data outside your systems. If you need us to sign an NDA or MSA before kickoff, we sign. If you need us to work under your security review, we work under it.

Who is on my account?

Hassan (Founder) and one GTM engineer. Two people, always. Every call has both. Every workflow build has both. If one is unavailable the other covers.

How is this different from an AI SDR product like 11x or Artisan?

AI SDR products are software you rent to send emails on their infrastructure. We are a team you rent to build the data, signal, and personalization layer behind whatever sending infrastructure you already run. You keep control of copy, cadence, and reply routing.
