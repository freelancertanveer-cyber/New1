CUSTOMIZATION BY PERSONA
================================================================================

CHARTER MANAGERS (shipping company staff, 50-200 vessel fleets):
- Pain: 18-day contract gaps, manual research
- Signal: Vessel employment events (AIS pings)
- ROI: Cut contract gap from 18 days to 6-8
- Proof: "Most managers are processing manually; we automate it"

VESSEL OPERATORS (independent owners, 5-30 vessels):
- Pain: 25% capacity underutilization, broker lag
- Signal: AIS data + broker intelligence + market timing
- ROI: Cut underutilization from 25% to 10% = $280k+ per vessel annually
- Proof: "Peer operators in your market average 88%; you're at 72%"

BROKERS (matching owners to charterers):
- Pain: 18-day contract cycle, low deal velocity
- Signal: Real-time vessel + opportunity matching
- ROI: Handle 3x contracts with same staff
- Proof: "Brokers with signal-fed leads close 35% more contracts"
