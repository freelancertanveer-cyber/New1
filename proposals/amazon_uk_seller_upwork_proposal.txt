10,000 Amazon UK sellers is easy to scrape and hard to make useful. The difference is whether each row ties the listing to the actual owner and a work email that reaches them, not a support desk.

I attached a 50-row sample from a recent Amazon build so you can judge the depth before spending a penny. Every row carries: ASIN + listing URL, brand, est. monthly + 12-month revenue, category, rating, company domain, decision-maker name, job title, LinkedIn, and work email.

Two things most applicants will miss on this job:

- Revenue sizing. When you can see one seller does 60k/month and another does 5k, you pitch the top 20% first instead of treating 10,000 rows as one blob.
- Category weighting. Amazon UK clusters differently than US. Confirm your target categories up front or 40% of the list lands off-target.
