## What I heard on the call

You're building the sales platform for insurance reps. Product side is solid: dialer, CRM, tools, everything on top of the rep's operation. Mission is to create more entrepreneurs and help them succeed as the tech landscape shifts.

Right now the ICP is non-captive life insurance agents in the US. 1099 contractors, running their own brokerages, MLM structure. They earn commission on their own policy sales, on downline recruit sales, and on recruiting bonuses. Two-sided customer base: consumers they sell to, and reps they recruit. Product mix is whole life (final expense, ~$1k/mo, savings-account style) and term life (~$100/mo pure protection). Distinguishing them from captive agents (Global Life, Primerica, etc.) is the hard filter, and LinkedIn Sales Nav alone doesn't do it.

The channel picture: Meta is already working since your ICP lives on Instagram and TikTok. You want LinkedIn and cold email opened as new sources of pipeline and downline recruits. You ran a GTM Engineer ad and are running interviews to lead this.

Your explicit concerns:

1. **Compliance.** You operate in SaaS compliance yourself. US is quietly pushing enforcement (FTC/FBI fingerprinting, evidence build, 2026-2027 fines). Cold outbound needs to stay inside common US market practice, not push it.
2. **Speed and burn.** If a channel doesn't work, no crisis. But you don't want to waste months in warmup and setup only to find out.
3. **Ownership.** Anything we build should be yours, so if we part ways nothing walks with me.

Roadmap-wise, non-captive insurance is the deep-first niche. You'll broaden later to high-ticket sales, info agencies, AI agencies, recruitment agencies. That means whatever we build for insurance should be portable to the next niche.

## What I'm proposing

A 2-month test phase with rigorous angle testing, followed by a retainer once we find message-market fit. Focus is speed to signal, not perfection of setup.

### Phase 1 — Test (Months 1-2)

**Data**
- Google Maps extraction for non-captive agent contacts. My brother's team runs the same play for a similar client at 3M sends/mo. The data does not sit in Apollo, ZoomInfo, or Cognism, so you are not fighting for the same inboxes as every other outbound team.
- Enrichment logic to filter captive vs non-captive. Captive companies (Global Life, Primerica, and the rest of the list) get excluded. LinkedIn Sales Nav's insurance filter is not enough on its own for this cut.

**Infrastructure**
- 270 pre-warmed mailboxes at $1.50/inbox/mo.
- Live campaigns in 4-5 days instead of 14-20 days manual warmup.
- Sender domains redirect to stacking.cash. Signature framing (business consultant, insurance consultant, whichever fits the copy).
- BCC your main company mailbox on replies. Leads land in your team's inbox from Day 1, not held hostage in the pre-warmed system.

**Automation**
- Claude Code + MCP driving the sequencer.
- Angle testing runs in parallel: multiple audience slices, copy versions, offers. Tens of variations without the manual click-through Clay operators charge for.
- You get a Claude Code seat. Your team can chat with the system directly, generate reports, tweak angles.

**Weekly output**
- 3-5k verified non-captive agent contacts added
- 20-30k sends
- Replies routed to your inbox via BCC
- Friday report: what's converting, what's not, next test to run

### Phase 2 — Scale (Month 3+)

Once we find a winning angle (positive reply volume and booked meetings), transition to retainer. Move to owned domains and inboxes if you want, or keep pre-warmed for pace. You own the infrastructure either way.

## Compliance Stance

US cold email is a gray area, not a black one. What we practice:

- Sender identity present in the email
- One-click unsubscribe honored
- No misleading subject lines or from-fields
- No address harvesting
- No GDPR geographies touched

That covers CAN-SPAM. On the enforcement pattern you raised (fingerprinting, 6-12 month evidence, 2027 fines): worth watching. I am active in the cold email mastermind community and none of the enforcement discussion you mentioned is landing there yet. We stay inside the practices that community treats as safe. If your internal bar is stricter, we adjust.
