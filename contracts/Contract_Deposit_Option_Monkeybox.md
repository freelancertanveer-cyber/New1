## 2. Scope of Work

The Provider will source, verify and deliver a B2B lead dataset with the following specification:

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Data source** | Google Maps |
| **Geography** | United States |
| **Target categories** | Residential Builder, General Contractor, Design & Build, Home Builders |
| **Volume** | 200,000 records |
| **Fields** | Business name, owner/decision-maker name (where available), verified business/owner email, phone number, website, full address, business category, and other available enrichment |

The Provider will apply multi-layer verification (Google API checks, website validation, and catch-all email verification) to maximise data quality.

The Provider targets **90%+ accuracy**. A margin of up to 10% may occur due to external source glitches outside the Provider's control. Any records flagged as inaccurate during the review period (Section 5) will be corrected or replaced free of charge.
